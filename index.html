研究焦點Research Focus

藥師與臨床醫學碩士生,研究工作聚焦於真實世界證據與實證統合的交會點。方法學涵蓋系統性回顧與統合分析、貝氏網絡統合分析,以及目標試驗模擬,應用領域包含腫瘤學(HR+/HER2- 乳癌之 CDK4/6 抑制劑)、移植醫學(以 everolimus 為基礎之免疫抑制方案)與神經退化疾病。目前正積極尋求藥物流行病學相關博士訓練機會,期望以大型理賠與登錄資料庫延伸此研究方向。 Pharmacist and clinical-medicine graduate researcher working at the intersection of real-world evidence and evidence synthesis. Methodological work spans systematic review & meta-analysis, Bayesian network meta-analysis, and target trial emulation, applied to oncology (CDK4/6 inhibitors in HR+/HER2- breast cancer), transplant medicine (everolimus-based immunosuppression), and neurodegenerative disease. Currently seeking doctoral training in pharmacoepidemiology to extend this work using large-scale claims and registry data.
